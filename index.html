About

The "Concept as Act" series develops a philosophical and formal framework for rethinking cognition, judgment, agency, and responsibility in the age of artificial intelligence.

It is grounded in the Triadic Theory of Action (TTA) — a dynamic model of cognition as a threefold act of Selection, Identification, and Anticipation — and the Theory of Judgment as an Act of Reality Constitution (JARC), which treats judgment not as a truth-functional evaluation but as a teleological act that actively constitutes reality.
